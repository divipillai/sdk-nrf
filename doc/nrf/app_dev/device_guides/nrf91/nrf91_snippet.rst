.. _ug_nrf91_snippet:

Snippets for an nRF91 Series device
###################################

The nRF91 Series' :ref:`app_build_snippets` are tailored for tracing on the nRF91 Series devices but can work with other boards as well.
They are documented together with the other |NCS| snippets on the :ref:`ug_snippets` page.

See the following pages for the snippets that target the nRF91 Series devices:

* :ref:`snippet-nrf91-modem-trace-ext-flash` - Stores modem traces to a dedicated partition on the external flash.
* :ref:`snippet-nrf91-modem-trace-uart` - Stores modem traces over the UART trace backend.
* :ref:`snippet-nrf91-modem-trace-rtt` - Stores modem traces over the RTT trace backend.
* :ref:`snippet-nrf91-modem-trace-ram` - Stores modem traces to a dedicated partition on the RAM.
* :ref:`snippet-tfm-enable-share-uart` - Directs the TF-M logs to the UART used by the application.
